# CropMLP on ViT-Tiny DINO backbones
### Update — 2026-06-04
- Three backbones, three CropMLPs, one CV protocol.
- Goal: does a 4× smaller DINO backbone match the production v1 result?

---

## Outline

1. **V1 baseline** — ViT-Small DINO (the production model)
2. **Two new variants** — ViT-Tiny @ 5K and 10K crops/experiment cap
3. **Direct comparison** — same per-crop MLP, same 5-fold strain holdout
4. **Diagnostic probe** — what happens if we drop EC35 entirely?

---

# Part 1: V1 ViT-Small DINO

---

## V1 — Backbone architecture

- ViT-Small/16 — embed_dim **384**, depth **12**, num_heads **6**, head_dim 64
- Input: 1-channel 128×128 grayscale crops (reflection-padded, `BORDER_REFLECT_101`)
- `mlp_ratio` 4.0, `drop_path_rate` 0.1, not time-conditioned
- **Backbone parameters: 21,418,368** (≈ 21.42 M)

---

## V1 — DINO pretraining configuration

- **Optimiser**: AdamW, `base_lr` 5e-4 → `min_lr` 1e-6, batch 64, 100 epochs, 10-epoch warmup, grad clip 3.0
- **Weight decay**: 0.04 → 0.4 (linear schedule)
- **Teacher EMA**: 0.996 → 1.0
- **Temperatures**: teacher 0.04 → 0.07 over 30 epochs, student 0.1
- **Centering**: momentum 0.9
- **Multi-crop**: 2 global @ 128 (scale 0.7–1.0) + 6 local @ 64 (scale 0.3–0.6)
- **DINO head**: hidden 2048, bottleneck 256, **4096 prototypes**, 3 layers (~6.56 M params)
- **Augmentation** (reduced from DINO defaults — defaults collapse this dataset):
  - `aug_brightness` **0.03** (not 0.3), `aug_contrast` 0.3, noise σ ≤ 0.01, defocus ≤ 3
  - CLAHE on, normalisation mean 0.3387 / std 0.1173

---

## V1 — Pretraining dataset and outcome

- Per-experiment cap: `max_crops_per_experiment = 5000`
- Older corpus: **≈ 207,000 effective training crops**
- **Best loss 0.815 at epoch 43**
- Checkpoint: `checkpoints/dino/best_backbone.pt`

---

## V1 — CropMLP head + cross-validation protocol

- **MLP architecture**: `Linear(384→128) → LayerNorm → GELU → Dropout(0.3) → Linear(128→64) → LayerNorm → GELU → Dropout(0.3) → Linear(64→2)`
- **CropMLP parameters: 58,050**
- **Training filter**: only crops with `rel_ts ≥ 2400 s` (≥ 40 min exposure), focused-only
- **Optimiser**: AdamW lr=1e-3, wd=0.01, batch 2048, 100 epochs, cosine schedule, val-AUROC early-stop (patience 15)
- **CV**: 5-fold strain holdout (seed 42, 2 R + 2 R strains held out per fold)

# Part 2: Two new ViT-Tiny variants

---

## What changed (and what didn't)

- **Same** input size, depth, patch size, MLP ratio, drop path, augmentation, optimiser, schedule, multi-crop, DINO head, normalisation, MLP head, CV protocol
- **Changed**: `embed_dim` 384 → **192**, `num_heads` 6 → **3** — both Tiny variants
- **Additionally for Tiny 10K**: `max_crops_per_experiment` 5,000 → **10,000**
- Tiny was trained from scratch by `train_vit_tiny_scratch.py`, forked from the V1 reproduction script

---

## ViT-Tiny 5K — configuration & outcome

- Architecture: embed_dim **192**, depth 12, num_heads **3**, head_dim 64
- **Backbone parameters: 5,400,768** (≈ 5.40 M) → ≈ 4× smaller than V1
- `max_crops_per_experiment = 5000` (same cap as V1)
- Corpus grew to 53 HDF5 experiments → **261,732 effective training crops** (~26% more than V1's older corpus)
- **Best loss 0.989 at epoch 32**
- Checkpoint: `checkpoints/dino_vit_tiny/dino/best_backbone.pt`

---

## ViT-Tiny 10K — configuration & outcome

- Same architecture as Tiny 5K (5.40 M backbone params)
- `max_crops_per_experiment = 10000` (doubled)
- 53 experiments × 10K cap → **516,732 effective training crops** (~2.5× v1)
- **Best loss 0.926 at epoch 27**
- Checkpoint: `checkpoints/dino_vit_tiny_10k/dino/best_backbone.pt`

---

## Backbone parameter comparison

| Model | embed_dim | num_heads | Backbone params | CropMLP params |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| ViT-Small v1 | 384 | 6 | **21,418,368** | 58,050 |
| ViT-Tiny 5K | 192 | 3 | **5,400,768** | 33,474 |
| ViT-Tiny 10K | 192 | 3 | **5,400,768** | 33,474 |

→ Tiny is **~4× smaller** at the backbone (75% fewer params) for the same downstream feature pipeline.

---

## DINO training-crop comparison

| Model | Cap / exp | HDF5 files | Effective crops | Best epoch | Best loss |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| ViT-Small v1 | 5,000 | ≈ 42 | **≈ 207,000** | 43 | **0.815** |
| ViT-Tiny 5K | 5,000 | 53 | **261,732** | 32 | 0.989 |
| ViT-Tiny 10K | 10,000 | 53 | **516,732** | 27 | **0.926** |

DINO loss is not directly comparable across architectures (different prototype distributions); downstream task is the right comparison.

## Interpretation 1 — AUROC is invariant

- All three feature spaces induce the **same ranking** of held-out experiments by mean P(R) on every fold.
- 4× backbone shrink + different DINO loss → still 0.7644 ± 0.141 AUROC.
- The population-mean discriminative signal that AST extracts is robust to representation swap of this magnitude.

---

## Interpretation 2 — Accuracy near-invariant; fold 2 is the wobble

- v1 and Tiny 5K: byte-identical accuracy (0.5970 ± 0.1030).
- Tiny 10K: loses one fold-2 experiment across the 0.5 threshold (acc 0.455 → 0.364).
- Fold-2 AUROC stays at 0.533 — underlying ranking is unchanged, only the decision boundary lands between two experiments differently.
- With 10–12 test experiments per fold, accuracy is quantised — moving one experiment matters a lot.

---

## Interpretation 3 — Fold 2 is intrinsically hard

- Held out R = EC35 + EC87, S = EC33 + EC39 — worst strain pairing in the rotation.
- All three backbones get AUROC = 0.5333 exactly.
- Failure mode is structural to that pairing, not a feature-quality issue.
- Likely needs broader strain coverage in train, or a different population statistic than naive mean P(R).

---

## Interpretation 4 — 10K of pretraining crops didn't help downstream

- DINO best loss improved 0.989 → 0.926 with the extra crops.
- But downstream AUROC was already saturated at the 5K cap.
- No evidence that ViT-Tiny is data-limited on this dataset; the extra crops sharpen invariances that aren't the R/S bottleneck.

# Part 4: Diagnostic probe — drop EC35

---

## Motivation

- The original CV fold 2 (R = EC35, EC87 / S = EC33, EC39) collapsed for **all three** backbones — AUROC 0.5333 exactly.
- Fold 3, the other fold containing EC35 in the holdout, also underperformed.
- Hypothesis: **EC35 is a confounder**. Its DINO embedding distribution may not separate cleanly from S strains at the population level.
- Test: train a fresh CropMLP on the Tiny 5K features with EC35 completely removed from train, val, and test.

---

## Method

- Added `--exclude-strains EC35` to `scripts/strain_holdout_crop_classifier.py` (drops the named strains before fold generation).
- Same Tiny 5K backbone, same `features_tiny_5k/`, same CropMLP architecture and training hyperparameters, same seed (42).
- 3 R experiments are dropped (all EC35).
- Fold rotation is drawn from a strictly smaller R pool → **fold compositions differ** from the standard run (this is the main caveat).

| Fold | Standard Tiny 5K | **No-EC35 run** |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| 0 | R=EC58,EC87 / S=EC36,EC39 | R=EC48,EC87 / S=EC33,EC42 |
| 1 | R=EC58,EC60 / S=EC33,EC39 | R=EC60,EC65 / S=EC39,EC79 |
| 2 | R=EC35,EC87 / S=EC33,EC39 | R=EC60,EC65 / S=EC36,EC79 |
| 3 | R=EC35,EC87 / S=EC36,EC39 | R=EC48,EC87 / S=EC42,EC89 |
| 4 | R=EC40,EC48 / S=EC39,EC67 | R=EC40,EC48 / S=EC33,EC79 |

---

## Headline result — EC35 excluded

| Metric | Standard Tiny 5K | **No-EC35 Tiny 5K** | Δ |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Experiment AUROC | 0.7644 ± 0.1410 | **0.9720 ± 0.0254** | **+0.208** |
| Experiment accuracy | 0.5970 ± 0.1030 | **0.6418 ± 0.0660** | +0.045 |

- AUROC up by 21 points; std drops by ~6×.
- **Two of five folds now achieve perfect ranking (AUROC = 1.000)**.
- Removing one strain accounts for nearly all the dispersion in the standard CV.

## Diagnostic — what this tells us

- **EC35 is the dominant difficulty in the original CV.** Removing it collapses both the mean error and the variance.
- AUROC 0.972 means the model **ranks experiments correctly** on the EC35-free population — the representations are good.
- But accuracy is still only 0.642 → **the 0.5 threshold is mis-calibrated**. Per-experiment mean P(R) doesn't straddle 0.5 cleanly; Platt scaling or a learned threshold should close most of the gap.

---

## Caveat (don't oversell)

- This is **not a clean A/B on the same test sets**. Removing EC35 from the R pool reshuffles the seeded `(R-combo, S-combo)` rotation — all five folds now have different `(R, S)` compositions.
- What we've shown is: *on a different distribution of held-out strains that doesn't include EC35, the same backbone + MLP achieve much higher AUROC*.
- To make a stronger claim ("EC35 itself is the confounder") we'd need a fixed-folds ablation: drop EC35 only from train (still test it) or only from test (still train on it).

---

# Recommendations

- **Adopt the ViT-Tiny (5K cap) backbone** for downstream R/S classification — same AUROC as v1, 4× fewer params, 4× cheaper inference per crop.
- **Investigate EC35.** It's responsible for most of the variance in the standard CV. Worth checking against the raw imaging — chip / focus / brightness drift, MIC outlier, contamination, mis-labelling? (See `april23_findings.md` for prior brittleness analysis.)
- **Calibrate the decision threshold.** AUROC 0.97 with accuracy 0.64 says the ranking is fine but the cutoff is wrong. Try Platt scaling or a held-out threshold sweep.
- **Skip retraining at higher pretraining crop budgets.** Tiny 10K (517K crops) did not help downstream. The model is not data-limited at the 5K cap.
